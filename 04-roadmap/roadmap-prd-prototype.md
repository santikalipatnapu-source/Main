# Feature Roadmap, Module 4 · StreamLine Spotlight

**Team:** 2 engineers + 1 designer

## Strategic anchors
- **Persona:** The Already-Lost User
- **Primary metric:** Wants his evenings filled with a small number of well-chosen films, not a large catalog to sift through himself.
- **Moment of misery:** UXR-04 — "I cancelled last year. It became a warehouse. Volume went up, quality of my evenings went down. A competitor emails me two hand-picked films a week and I watch both." Tom is the direct, literal proof of your hook's thesis: he didn't just get frustrated, he left and found better curation elsewhere. He's not a warning sign — he's the outcome your hook is warning about, already realized.
- **Guardrail:** Maintain or improve overall mobile Start‑Playing rate so that reducing discovery friction does not unintentionally lower the number of users who begin a session.

## Roadmap
### NOW, 3-week sprint
- **A6 Spotlight Digest Email**, Delivers the exact experience Tom defected for—short, high‑quality picks delivered proactively.

### NEXT, following 1-2 sprints
- **A1 Spotlight Curated Rail**, Directly answers Tom’s misery by replacing the “warehouse” feel with top‑of‑home curation he can trust.
- **A5 Personalized Spotlight Queue**, Creates Tom’s competitor‑like experience: a persistent feed of a few hand‑chosen films he can trust weekly.
- **A9 Advanced Filter Engine**, Helps narrow choices but still requires Tom to do the work—useful, but not core to the “few perfect picks” problem.

### LATER, backlog
- **A3 Hidden Gem Badge**, Cute signal, but adds more surface options rather than fewer deeply curated picks.
- **A10 Offline Download (Spotlight)**, Helps retain commitment once he picks a Spotlight film, but does not reduce the discovery load upfront.

### ✂ Cut List
- **A2 'Why You'll Love This' Label**, Reduces decision paralysis, but does not materially reduce the volume of choices Tom must sift through.
- **A4 Mood-Based Entry Point**, Adds a new choice step rather than eliminating them; does not reduce “warehouse” overload.
- **A7 Curator Profiles**, Adds depth, not simplicity; Tom wants fewer decisions, not more editorial surfaces.
- **A8 Watch Party (Spotlight)**, Social viewing does not solve Tom’s curation crisis and is extremely heavy for a 2‑engineer team.
