Tom, the already-lost user

A 52-year-old, long-tenured subscriber who cancelled after volume outpaced curation — and found a competitor's twice-weekly hand-picked email a better fit for his evenings.

Strategy: Restore brand authority and win back users by prioritizing human-led curation and restoration over mass-market scale.

Competitive advantages over Tom's manual workaround

01
